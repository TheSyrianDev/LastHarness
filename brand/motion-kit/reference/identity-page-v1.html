LastHarness · brand identity · version 1

The last harness you'll need.

A worker that looks at the slot where its tool goes. That is the whole mark: a face, a glance, and a place for the work. It came from Round 8 as Glance + Slot, and it is used here exactly as it was drawn, fitted inside a circle.

01 THE MARK
Construction

Built on a 64 grid, inside a circle

Every part is a whole number or a simple half, so the mark can be redrawn from these numbers alone.

The rules

  • Circle: radius 30 around the centre of the 64 grid. The slot touches it; the face sits inside it with room to spare.
  • Face: a 38 × 38 square with corners of radius 15, from (8, 18) to (46, 56).
  • Slot: a circle of radius 6 at (49, 15). Its gap to the face's corner is 4.5.
  • Eyes: two pills. The near eye is 6 × 11, the far eye is 4.6 × 10.2, because it is turned away from us.
  • Glance: both eyes move 2.4 right and 2 up, and tilt 8° toward the slot, around the centre of the face.
  • Clear space: the slot's diameter (12) on every side. Nothing else enters that space.
Sizes

From a poster to a 16-pixel tray icon

Above 32 pixels the mark is drawn as it is. From 32 down to 20 the distance version opens the eyes and grows the slot, so they survive. At 16 pixels a hand-placed pixel version takes over, with every pixel on the grid.

02 WORDMARK AND LOCKUPS
Wordmark

A custom lowercase, round like the face

Drawn with one stroke weight, round ends and circular bowls, so the letters share the face's softness. "last" and "harness" can split into two colours, cream and lichen, which also shows where the name breaks. The Arabic is set in Readex Pro for now; a custom Arabic wordmark is still to come from a type designer.

03 COLOUR
Grove

Four colours for the brand, four for status

Grove is a pine and cognac blend: forest and moss for depth, lichen as the brand colour, cream as paper. The status colours only ever appear in the slot, so the slot can tell you what a worker is doing at a glance.

Omarchy

On Omarchy, the mark takes the theme's colours

The spec says everything follows the Omarchy theme. The mark needs only three colours: the face takes the theme's foreground, the eyes its background, and the slot its accent. Because the mark is a silhouette, it survives any theme. The colours below are close approximations of each theme.

04 MOODS AND MOTION
Moods

The eyes carry the state of the harness

Seven moods, each tied to something the product actually does. Only the eyes and the slot change; the face never does.

Motion

Five animations, all from the same three parts

The rule, as with the bots: the work moves first, then the face looks at it.

05 THE CREW
Workers

The logo is a worker with an empty slot

Each worker is a ball in its own colour, and its tool sits exactly where the logo's slot is: up and to the right. When the tool starts to work, the worker turns and looks up at it. The logo is the harness itself: the same glance, waiting for whichever tool the job needs.

06 PATTERN AND ICONS
Pattern

A field of slots, with a few faces looking at them

For backgrounds, packaging and the website. The dots are slots; every so often one has a face beside it, glancing at it.
