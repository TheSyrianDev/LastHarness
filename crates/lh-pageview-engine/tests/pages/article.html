A plain article about local-first software

By Test Author · 11 October 2026 · 6 min read

Local-first software keeps your data on your own machine and treats the network as an optional helper. The idea is old, but it is becoming practical as devices get faster and storage gets cheaper. This page is a stand-in for a normal article: headings, long paragraphs, a table, a quote, inline code and a styled box.

Why it matters

When data lives with you, the program keeps working on a plane, in a tunnel, or when a company closes. Sync becomes a feature instead of a requirement. Privacy follows from the design rather than from a promise in a policy page.

The best network is the one you do not need for the thing you are doing right now.
